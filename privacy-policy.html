Privacy Policy

Vertex Music Co. (India)
Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. About this app

"Vertex Music Publisher" is an internal tool operated by Vertex Music Co. It is used only to publish and schedule our own marketing content (product photos, videos and captions) on the official Vertex Music Facebook Page and Instagram business account (@vertexmusic.india).

2. Information we collect

This app does not collect personal information from the public, website visitors, customers or followers. The app only accesses the following business information, and only for our own accounts:

  • The IDs of our Facebook Page and Instagram business account.
  • Access credentials (tokens) that allow the app to publish on our own accounts.
  • The content we choose to publish (images, videos and captions).

3. How we use information

We use this information only to publish and schedule posts on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and to record whether each post was published successfully.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties, except with Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) as needed to publish our own content.

5. Storage and security

Access credentials are stored in secure, encrypted secret storage and are never published or shared. Access can be revoked at any time from our Meta Business Settings.

6. Data deletion

This app does not store personal data about members of the public. If you believe we hold any information about you and want it deleted, email us at niketan.vertexmusic@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will respond and delete the information within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
